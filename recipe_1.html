Red Lentil Soup (Mercimek Çorbası)

⏱ 45 Mins | 👤 4-6 Servings | 🔥 320 kcal | 🥘 Easy
Red lentil soup is a staple in Turkish cuisine, widely enjoyed across Turkey in both homes and local restaurants. Often served as a starter or a light meal, this nutritious soup is known for its silky texture and earthy flavor. It is naturally vegan and high in plant-based protein.

Ingredients

  • 1 ½ cups split red lentils (thoroughly rinsed)
  • 1 medium yellow onion (finely chopped)
  • 1 medium carrot (grated or finely cubed)
  • 1 medium potato (cubed)
  • 6 cups vegetable broth or chicken broth
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 tablespoon Turkish pepper paste (biber salçası) - (Sub: 1 tbsp tomato paste + ½ tsp paprika)
  • 1 teaspoon salt (or to taste)
  • ½ teaspoon black pepper
  • ½ teaspoon cumin (optional, for authentic flavor)
  • For serving: 1 tablespoon butter, ½ teaspoon Aleppo pepper or chili flakes, 1 teaspoon dried mint, and lemon wedges.

Instructions

  1. In a large pot, heat the olive oil over medium heat. Add the onion, carrot, and potato. Sauté for 5-7 minutes until the onions are translucent.
  2. Stir in the pepper paste and cumin, if using. Cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
  3. Add the rinsed split red lentils and the vegetable broth. Bring to a boil.
  4. Reduce the heat to low and simmer for 20-25 minutes, or until the lentils and vegetables are completely soft.
  5. Blend the soup until smooth using an immersion blender. If the soup is too thick, add a splash of hot water to reach your desired consistency.
  6. Season with salt and black pepper to taste.
  7. For serving: In a small pan, melt the butter with the chili flakes and dried mint until sizzling. Drizzle over the soup and serve with fresh lemon wedges.
