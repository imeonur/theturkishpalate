Red Lentil Soup (Mercimek Corbasi)

Red lentil soup is a staple in Turkish cuisine, widely enjoyed across Turkey in both homes and local restaurants. Often served as a starter or a light meal, this nutritious soup is known for its silky texture and earthy flavor.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups split red lentils
  • 1 medium yellow onion
  • 1 medium carrot
  • 6 cups vegetable broth
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon salt & pepper

Instructions
