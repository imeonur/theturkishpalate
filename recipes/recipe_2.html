Turkish Stuffed Eggplant (Karniyarik)

Karniyarik is one of the most beloved dishes in Turkish home cooking. Its name refers to the slit made in the eggplants, which are then filled with a savory mixture of meat and vegetables.

Ingredients

  • 4 medium globe eggplants
  • 1/2 lb (225g) ground beef
  • 2 medium tomatoes
  • 1 tablespoon Turkish pepper paste (biber salcasi)
  • Olive oil for roasting and filling

Instructions

  1. Peel eggplants in a zebra pattern and soak in salted water.
